What Is an ATS Score?

Most companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to automatically scan resumes before a human ever sees them. PLUTO analyzes your resume the same way — checking keywords, formatting, skills, and experience — and gives you a score out of 100 so you know exactly where you stand.

🎯

Jobs Matched to You

Once your resume is analyzed, PLUTO compares your skills and experience against real job listings and ranks them by match percentage — so you spend less time searching and more time applying to roles you're actually qualified for.
